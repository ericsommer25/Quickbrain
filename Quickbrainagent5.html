Outliner ⤢

1. Single Click a topic to select it.

2. Double-click a topic to edit it.

3. Use keyboard arrow keys to navigate the topics in the outliner.

4. Use Ctrl + Arrow Keys to move a topic up, down, left, or right in the outliner.

5. Important: Click the number of a topic to hide/show its child levels.

6. Important: Click the robot icon in the bar to open/close the AI Chatbot.

Note: Every topic has its own 'page' in the word processor. Content entered into the word processor is automatically appended to the currently selected outliner topic. That content will appear again whenever you select that topic in the future.
